Custom-designed and fabricated panel enclosure for the Structural Health Monitoring System at Masjid Al-Jabbar, Bandung, Indonesia. The panel is engineered to securely house and protect monitoring equipment, electrical components, and control systems while supporting reliable system operation and ease of maintenance.
